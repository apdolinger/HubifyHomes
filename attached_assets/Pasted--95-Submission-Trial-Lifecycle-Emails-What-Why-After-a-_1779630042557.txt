#95 - Submission Trial Lifecycle Emails

What & Why

After a user submits the Client Submission Form and starts a 30-day demo/trial experience, Hubify Homes should automatically communicate key milestones in the trial lifecycle.

This includes:
- immediate welcome/activation email,
- 7-day expiration warning,
- trial expiration + billing activation notice.

The goal is to create a professional onboarding flow and improve conversion from trial to paid client.

Done looks like

- A welcome email is automatically sent immediately after a successful submission when the user selects:
  - “Interested in 30-day free trial”
  OR
  - “Ready to start onboarding”

- A scheduled email sends 7 days before trial expiration
- A scheduled email sends when the trial expires
- Trial dates are stored in the database
- Emails use the existing email provider configuration (Resend or SendGrid)
- Emails use branded Hubify Homes styling and professional formatting
- Failed email sends are logged safely without breaking submission flow

Out of scope

- Stripe billing automation
- Subscription cancellation logic
- Customer self-service billing portal
- In-app notifications
- SMS notifications

Steps

1. Database Changes

Extend onboarding_prospects with:

- trialStartedAt (timestamp)
- trialEndsAt (timestamp)
- trialStatus (varchar)

Trial statuses:
- inactive
- active
- expiring_soon
- expired
- converted

If submission intent includes:
- “Interested in 30-day free trial”
OR
- “Ready to start onboarding”

Then:
- automatically set:
  - trialStartedAt = now()
  - trialEndsAt = now() + 30 days
  - trialStatus = "active"

2. Welcome Email

Immediately after successful submission:
Send a welcome email to the submitter.

Subject:
Welcome to Hubify Homes — Your 30-Day Demo Has Started

Email content should:
- thank them for their submission,
- confirm their 30-day demo access,
- explain that the team will review their submission,
- explain onboarding/demo next steps,
- reinforce the platform value,
- provide support contact information,
- encourage replying with questions.

Suggested messaging tone:
Professional, modern SaaS onboarding experience.

3. 7-Day Expiration Warning

Create a scheduled job/cron process that runs daily.

When:
- current date is within 7 days of trialEndsAt
AND
- trialStatus = active

Then:
- send warning email,
- update trialStatus = expiring_soon

Subject:
Your Hubify Homes Demo Ends Soon

Email should explain:
- their demo expires in 7 days,
- onboarding/billing will begin afterward,
- encourage them to finalize setup,
- encourage contacting support with questions.

4. Trial Expired Email

When:
- current date >= trialEndsAt
AND
- trialStatus IN ("active", "expiring_soon")

Then:
- send expiration email,
- update trialStatus = expired

Subject:
Your Hubify Homes Demo Has Ended

Email should explain:
- their 30-day demo period has ended,
- their organization is now transitioning into billing/onboarding,
- support is available for questions,
- next steps if billing/setup is incomplete.

5. Email Templates

Create reusable email template helpers:
- sendTrialWelcomeEmail()
- sendTrialExpiringEmail()
- sendTrialExpiredEmail()

Use existing branded email styling if available.

6. Scheduler

Implement a lightweight daily scheduler:
- node-cron or existing scheduled task system
- runs once daily
- safely processes trial lifecycle emails
- prevents duplicate sends

7. Logging

Add server logging for:
- email send success/failure
- scheduled task execution
- number of trials processed

Relevant files

shared/schema.ts
server/routes.ts
server/runMigrations.ts
server/index.ts
server/email.ts (or equivalent)
