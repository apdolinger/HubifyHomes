# Hubify Homes Inc. Privacy Policy

**Version v1.2 · Effective June 2, 2026**

Hubify Homes Inc. ("Hubify," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information entrusted to us.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect information when you access or use the Hubify platform, website, applications, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

We may collect information you provide directly, including:

* Name
* Email address
* Phone number
* Company name
* Business address
* Account information
* Property information
* Client information
* Vendor information
* Communications with Hubify
* Support requests
* Documents and files uploaded to the Services

### Payment Information

Payment information is processed by third-party payment processors such as Stripe. Hubify does not store complete payment card information.

### Information Collected Automatically

We may automatically collect:

* IP address
* Browser type
* Device information
* Operating system
* Usage information
* Authentication activity
* Log files
* Cookie and session information

## 2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

* Provide and operate the Services
* Create and manage accounts
* Process subscriptions and payments
* Provide customer support
* Secure and maintain the platform
* Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents
* Improve existing features and develop new features
* Communicate service-related information
* Comply with legal obligations
* Enforce our agreements and policies

## 3. Aggregated and De-Identified Information

Hubify may create aggregated, anonymized, or de-identified information derived from use of the Services.

Such information may be used for:

* Analytics
* Benchmarking
* Product development
* Research
* Service improvements
* Industry insights
* Operational reporting

Hubify will not sell identifiable customer data or identifiable personal information to third parties.

## 4. How We Share Information

We may share information with service providers that assist in operating the Services, including providers of:

* Hosting and cloud infrastructure
* Payment processing
* Email delivery
* Authentication
* Customer support
* Analytics
* Artificial intelligence and automation services

We may also disclose information:

* When required by law
* To comply with legal process
* To protect rights, safety, or security
* To investigate fraud or misuse
* In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets

## 5. Artificial Intelligence Features

Certain features may utilize artificial intelligence, automation, machine learning, or third-party AI providers.

Information submitted to AI-powered features may be processed by third-party providers solely for the purpose of delivering requested functionality and operating the Services.

Users are responsible for determining whether information submitted to AI features is appropriate for their business requirements.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

* Provide the Services
* Comply with legal obligations
* Resolve disputes
* Enforce agreements
* Maintain business records

Generally:

* Account information is retained while accounts remain active.
* Billing and tax records may be retained as required by law.
* Security and audit logs may be retained for operational and security purposes.

## 7. Data Security

Hubify uses commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information.

Security measures may include:

* Encryption in transit
* Encryption at rest
* Access controls
* Authentication controls
* Monitoring and logging

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and Hubify cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Hubify may use cookies and similar technologies to:

* Authenticate users
* Maintain sessions
* Remember preferences
* Analyze usage patterns
* Improve Services

You may control cookie settings through your browser, although disabling certain cookies may affect functionality.

## 11. Children's Privacy

Hubify does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
