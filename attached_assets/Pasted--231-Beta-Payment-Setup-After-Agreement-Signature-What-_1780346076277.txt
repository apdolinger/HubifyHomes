#231 - Beta Payment Setup After Agreement Signature

What & Why

After an approved beta client signs the Beta Agreement, they should move to payment setup before the platform is initialized.

The payment step should collect payment through Stripe using the approved pricing already stored on the prospect record.

Done Looks Like

After the agreement is signed:

* stage = payment_pending
* agreementStatus = signed
* the onboarding page advances to Step 2: Payment Setup

Step 2 should display:

* Organization Name
* Portfolio Tier
* Number of Properties
* Original Monthly Price
* Beta Discount Percentage
* Discounted Monthly Price
* One-Time Database Initialization Fee
* Total Due Today

Payment Logic

Total due today should include:

* One-Time Database Initialization Fee
* First subscription payment, unless we decide subscription starts after platform activation

For now, use:

Total Due Today = Setup Fee + First Month Discounted Subscription

Stripe

Create a Stripe Checkout session for the approved beta prospect.

Checkout should include:

* Setup/database initialization fee as one-time charge
* Discounted monthly subscription as recurring subscription
* Customer email from the prospect record
* Organization/company name in metadata
* Prospect ID in metadata
* Portfolio tier in metadata
* Beta cohort number in metadata
* Discount percentage in metadata

After successful payment:

* paymentStatus = paid
* paymentCompletedAt = current timestamp
* stripeCustomerId = saved
* stripeSubscriptionId = saved
* stage = platform_initializing

If payment fails or is cancelled:

* stage remains payment_pending
* show clear retry message

Frontend

On /onboarding/:token, add Step 2: Payment Setup.

If agreement is not signed, Step 2 should be locked.

If agreement is signed but payment is not complete, show the payment summary and a button:

“Continue to Secure Payment”

After Stripe success redirect, show:

“Payment received. Your platform setup is now starting.”

Out of Scope

Actual platform/organization provisioning.
Importing homes.
Creating staff users.
Final welcome email.
