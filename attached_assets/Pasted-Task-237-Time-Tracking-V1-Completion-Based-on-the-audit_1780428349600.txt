Task #237 — Time Tracking V1 Completion

Based on the audit, time tracking is largely implemented.

Goal:
Complete the existing system so beta organizations can realistically use it for field staff management, billable work tracking, and future job costing.

Please focus only on completing and polishing the current time-tracking system.

Phase 1 Requirements

1. Work Type
   Add a workType field to time entries.

Suggested values:

* Inspection
* Home Watch Visit
* Maintenance
* Vendor Coordination
* Travel
* Administrative
* Cleaning
* Welcome Home Service
* Departure Service
* Other

Visible on:

* Clock-in flow
* Time entry edit screen
* Reports

2. Mileage Tracking
   Add optional mileage field.

Requirements:

* Miles driven
* Included in reports
* Stored on time entry
* Visible in admin review

No reimbursement calculations yet.

3. Approval Workflow
   Add status:

* Draft
* Pending Approval
* Approved
* Rejected

Requirements:

* Staff submit entries
* Managers approve/reject
* Approved entries become locked
* Rejected entries require correction

4. Missing Clock-Out Detection
   Flag entries that remain open beyond a configurable threshold.

Default:
12 hours

Show:

* Dashboard warning
* Time Tracking warning
* Admin notification

5. Overtime Detection
   Flag employees exceeding:

40 hours per week

Show:

* Badge in reports
* Warning in admin review

No payroll calculations.

6. Live PDF Reports
   Replace sample PDF generator.

Generate actual time reports from real data.

Phase 2 Requirements

7. Approved Time → Draft Invoice
   Allow managers to:

* Select approved billable entries
* Generate draft invoice
* Review invoice
* Send invoice

Do not auto-send.

Validation

Please provide:

* Screenshots
* End-to-end test results
* Approval workflow demonstration
* Invoice generation demonstration

Out of Scope

* QuickBooks integration
* Payroll integration
* Profitability dashboards
* Materials costing
* Photo capture
* Revenue analytics

These will be considered after beta feedback.
