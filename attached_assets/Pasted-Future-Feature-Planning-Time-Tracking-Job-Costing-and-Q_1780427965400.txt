Future Feature Planning: Time Tracking, Job Costing, and QuickBooks Integration

Do not build this yet. Please review the current Hubify codebase and advise how this should fit into the roadmap.

Goal:
Hubify should eventually help organizations track staff time, understand profitability by property, and optionally sync accounting/payroll data to QuickBooks.

Core concept:
Hubify should remain the operations platform. QuickBooks should remain the accounting/payroll system.

Potential future workflow:

1. Team Member Clock In / Clock Out
   Staff can clock in from Hubify or Field Mode and select:

* Property
* Task/service
* Work type
* Billable or non-billable
* Notes
* Start time
* End time
* Optional mileage
* Optional materials/costs

2. Manager Timesheet Review
   Admins can review:

* Hours by employee
* Hours by property
* Billable vs non-billable time
* Overtime flags
* Missing clock-outs
* Notes/photos tied to work performed

3. Job Costing by Property
   Hubify should calculate:

* Labor cost
* Mileage cost
* Material cost
* Vendor cost
* Total cost per property
* Revenue per property
* Profitability per property

4. Billable Time to Invoice
   For billable work:

* Approved time entries can generate draft invoice line items
* Example: Vendor Coordination — 2.5 hours × $85/hr = $212.50
* Admin reviews before sending invoice

5. QuickBooks Integration Later
   Eventually sync:

* Customers
* Employees
* Service items
* Invoices
* Payments
* Time activities
* Possibly payroll exports

Important:
QuickBooks should not receive operational data like inspections, reports, occupancy, property timelines, or internal tasks unless needed for billing/accounting.

Please audit what already exists around:

* Time tracking
* Clock in/out
* Billable flags
* Time reports
* Payroll exports
* Invoice line-item generation
* QuickBooks readiness

Then recommend:

1. What is already built
2. What needs cleanup
3. What should be added for Version 1
4. What should wait until Version 2
5. Whether QuickBooks should be before or after real beta feedback from Brittany and Trent

My current instinct:

* Time tracking/job costing could be Version 1.
* QuickBooks integration should wait until after beta feedback.
