#229 - Beta Approval Email & Agreement Initiation

What & Why

When a Super Admin clicks "Approve Beta Application", the applicant should immediately receive an approval email that starts onboarding.

Approval should not simply update internal status fields. It should begin the onboarding journey by directing the applicant to complete:

1. Beta Agreement
2. Payment Setup
3. Platform Initialization

Done Looks Like

When a Super Admin clicks "Approve Beta Application":

* Pricing is calculated.
* Beta cohort is assigned.
* Portfolio tier is assigned.
* Agreement status is set to Pending.
* An approval email is automatically sent.

If the email sends successfully:

* stage = agreement_pending
* approvalEmailSent = true
* approvalEmailSentAt is recorded

If the email fails:

* approval action should fail and notify the admin
* do not move the prospect to agreement_pending
* keep them in review status

Email Contents

The email should include:

Organization Name

Property Count

Assigned Portfolio Tier

Original Monthly Price

Discount Percentage

Discounted Monthly Price

One-Time Database Initialization Fee

Beta Cohort Number

A statement that beta pricing is locked in for the life of their active subscription while remaining in good standing.

The email should contain a prominent button:

"Start Onboarding"

The onboarding link should direct them to the upcoming agreement workflow.

Example URL:

https://hubifyhomesonline.com/onboarding/{secure-token}

Security

Generate a unique onboarding token.

Store:

* onboardingToken
* onboardingTokenCreatedAt
* onboardingTokenExpiresAt

The onboarding link should use this token rather than exposing prospect IDs.

Future Workflow

The onboarding page launched from the email will eventually include:

Step 1: Agreement Review & Signature

Step 2: Payment Method Setup

Step 3: Platform Initialization

Step 4: Welcome to Hubify Homes

Out of Scope

Agreement generation itself.
Stripe checkout implementation.
Organization provisioning.

Those will be subsequent tasks.
